# PharmaReg AI — pitch deck brief (all facts verified, nothing invented)

## Company
PharmaReg AI — AI regulatory-compliance audits for pharmaceutical manufacturers. Live at https://pharmareg-ai.fastapicloud.dev

## Problem
Pharma manufacturers in Pakistan and GCC face strict regulators (DRAP in Pakistan, SFDA in Saudi Arabia, EDE in UAE). Preparing for GMP inspections is manual, slow, and expensive; consultants charge $800–5,000 per engagement. Failed inspections mean halted production and lost licenses. Small/mid manufacturers can't afford Veeva/MasterControl.

## Solution / Product (LIVE, working)
- **Free DRAP GMP self-audit** (/audit): 48 questions across 10 WHO-GMP domains → 0–100 graded report with prioritized findings + corrective actions, print-friendly.
- **Audit Cloud SaaS** (/portal): accounts, 3 regulatory frameworks — DRAP (48 questions), SFDA Saudi (51 questions, grounded in SFDA GMP guide, Arabic labelling guide, eSDR), EDE UAE (51 questions). Plans: Free (3 audits), Pro $49/mo, Enterprise $199/mo. Unlisted shareable report links.
- **QMS Lite** (/portal/qms, Pro/Enterprise): document control, deviations, CAPA, change control, training records, hash-chained audit trail.
- Lead capture: audit-request form → WhatsApp (+92 311 6925020).

## Business model
SaaS subscriptions ($49/$199 per month) + single audits ($800–1,200) + quarterly retainers ($3,000–5,000). Manual billing today (bank transfer/WhatsApp).

## Market
Start: Pakistan's pharmaceutical manufacturers (DRAP-regulated). Expand: GCC — Saudi Arabia (SFDA) and UAE (EDE), where the product already has localized frameworks including Arabic labelling requirements.
